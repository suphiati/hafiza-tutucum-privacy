Brain Master — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

This policy covers the Brain Master app (called “Hafıza Tutucum” in Turkish) on iPhone and on Android. The two versions handle data differently, so each has its own section below.

iPhone (iOS): no ads, no account or sign-in, no tracking — we collect no data ourselves. Your notes, reminders and images stay on your device. The only data that leaves it is anonymous technical diagnostics from Google ML Kit when you use image-to-text (see below).

Android: your notes stay on your device by default. To stay free, the Android app shows ads from Google AdMob, and it offers an optional Google sign-in to back up to your own Google Drive.

iPhone app (iOS)

What data do we collect?

We collect nothing ourselves. Your notes, reminders and images never leave your device; they are not sent to any server or stored on the internet. The iOS app contains no advertising and no tracking, and it has no account or sign-in.

Google ML Kit diagnostics: image-to-text (OCR) uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device. When you use it, ML Kit sends Google anonymous technical diagnostics: device model, iOS and app version, performance metrics (such as processing time), error codes and a per-installation identifier that isn’t tied to you. Your images, their text and your notes are never sent. This data is not linked to you and is not used for tracking or advertising. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

AI features

All AI features — semantic search, the “Ask your notes” chat, summarization and image-to-text (OCR) — run on your device. Your notes are never sent anywhere for these operations.

The AI models are downloaded once, when you turn on an AI feature, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), a public model host. The download request contains nothing about you or your notes; after that the AI works offline.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders on time (scheduled on your device).
  • Camera and photos: to add images to your notes. Images are stored only on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition (voice notes): uses Apple’s speech recognition to turn your speech into text. Depending on your device and language, audio may be processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the app does not store audio or send it anywhere else.
  • Calendar: only when you tap “Add to calendar” on a reminder, to write the event to your phone’s calendar.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: only if you turn on App Lock. Authentication is handled by iOS; the app never sees your biometric data.
  • Internet: only to download the optional AI models. Your notes and the AI features work without an internet connection.

Data sharing

We do not share or sell anything — we don’t have access to your notes or data in the first place.

Backup and deletion

With “Export backup” (Settings → Backup), your data is written to a file you choose, for example in Files or iCloud Drive. You alone decide where to save or send it; the file is not accessible to us. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Children’s privacy

The iOS app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Android app

What data do we process?

Unless you turn on cloud backup yourself, your notes, reminders and images never leave your device; they are not sent to any server or stored on the internet. If you turn on cloud backup, a copy is written only to your own Google Drive (see “Account & backup” below).

The exception for ads: to show, measure and protect ads from abuse, Google AdMob may process your device’s advertising ID and standard ad data (e.g. approximate location/country, device type, ad interactions). The content of your notes is never shared with ad providers.

Image-to-text (OCR) uses Google ML Kit on your device; when you use it, ML Kit sends Google anonymous technical diagnostics (device model, versions, performance metrics, error codes, a per-installation identifier). Your images, their text and your notes are never sent.

AI features

All AI features — semantic search, the “Ask your notes” chat, summarization and image-to-text (OCR) — run on your device. Your notes are never sent anywhere for these operations. The AI models are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co).

Advertising

The Android app shows banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use identifiers such as the advertising ID to serve, personalize and protect ads against fraud. In regions such as the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent at app launch before personalized ads are shown (Google’s consent screen). You can manage ad personalization at any time in your phone’s settings — reset your advertising ID or turn off personalization. See how Google uses ad data here.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show reminders on time (on your device only).
  • Camera and gallery: to add images to your notes. Images are stored only on your device.
  • Microphone (voice notes): uses your device’s speech recognition service (e.g. Google) to transcribe your speech. Audio is processed by that service under its own privacy policy; the app does not store audio or send it anywhere else.
  • Calendar: only when you tap “Add to calendar” on a reminder, to write the event to your phone’s calendar.
  • Internet: to load ads, to download the optional AI models and (if you sign in) for cloud backup. Your notes and the AI features work without an internet connection.

Data sharing

We do not share or sell your notes and content to anyone — we don’t have access to them in the first place. Only for showing ads, the ad data described above is processed by Google AdMob.

Account & backup (optional)

With “Export backup”, your data is written to a file you choose; you alone decide where to save or send it, and the file is not accessible to us.

If you choose, you can sign in with Google and turn on cloud backup. In that case your Google name and email address are used in the app to identify your account, and your notes, reminders and images are written only to a private app folder (appDataFolder) in your own Google Drive. Only you and this app can access that folder; nothing is sent to our servers and we have no access to your data.

Signing in and cloud backup are entirely optional. You can sign out, stop backing up or delete your Drive backup at any time from Settings. Deletion steps (Turkish): Account and data deletion.

Children’s privacy

Because the Android app shows ads and processes an advertising ID, it is primarily intended for users aged 13 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the current version in the app and on this page.
